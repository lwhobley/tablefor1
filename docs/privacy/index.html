Privacy Policy

This page explains what Table for 2 collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to put into the app, such as your account details, saved meals, profile preferences, pantry items, and any content you submit. If you sign in, we also process your authentication data through our backend provider.

How we use information

We use your information to run the app, personalize recommendations, save your preferences, display your content, and improve the product. We also use it to provide account support and to keep the app functioning securely.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers that help us operate the app, including hosting, authentication, payment, and subscription-management providers that process data on our behalf.

Your choices

You can update or delete your account data in the app. You can also contact support if you want help accessing, correcting, or deleting your information.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app and meet legal, security, or operational requirements. If you delete your account, we remove or anonymize data where appropriate.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the support page.
